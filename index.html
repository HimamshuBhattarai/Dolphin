ABOUT US

Dolphin English School is a private school located in Godawari Municipality, Lalitpur district, within Bagmati province.We ensure to provide quality education, benchmarked to fully unlock the potential of the body, mind, and spirit.

Our learners are imparted skill-based knowledge through the framework of the latest curriculum, supplemented by modern approaches to teaching-learning pedagogy. We prioritize instilling in our learners a sense of discipline, good moral values and character, accountability, and responsibility towards contributing to a cleaner and greener planet, in addition to the development of the intellect, thereby upholding the essence of our school motto "DIVE DEEP, SOAR HIGH".
